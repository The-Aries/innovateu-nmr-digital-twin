# Scientific limitations

- RMDI is a relative metallic-sodium NMR signal ratio. The link between signal intensity and deposited mass depends on morphology and measurement physics. [`classify`](../src/detect.py) · [Team specification §11](../../references/external/innovateu_nmr_battery_health_proposal.md#11-final-scientific-limitations)
- Residual metallic sodium may include material that is still electrochemically recoverable. Other irreversible sodium losses can occur outside the metallic-sodium signal. The prototype observes the fitted metal and reference windows. [`fit` and `simulation`](../config.yaml) · [Team specification §11](../../references/external/innovateu_nmr_battery_health_proposal.md#11-final-scientific-limitations)
- A rising RMDI can trigger a qualitative accumulation warning. Quantifying failure probability or certifying operating safety would require independent evidence. The twin's output is a comparison, a conditional short-horizon forecast and a proposed next measurement. [`_apply_packet` and `_next_evidence`](../src/twin.py) · [Team specification §11](../../references/external/innovateu_nmr_battery_health_proposal.md#11-final-scientific-limitations)
- The synthetic trajectory tests the pipeline on one virtual cell. Bray et al. provide spectral context from a formation cycle. The assumed post-discharge accumulation schedule and forecast have no population or real-cell kinetics validation. [`trajectory_protocol_and_noise`](../config.yaml) · [Published basis](../README.md#scientific-reference-points) · [Team specification §11](../../references/external/innovateu_nmr_battery_health_proposal.md#11-final-scientific-limitations)
- The software records evidence and suggests measurements. Experimental decisions and safety controls remain with the responsible operator. [`_next_evidence`](../src/twin.py) · [Team specification §11](../../references/external/innovateu_nmr_battery_health_proposal.md#11-final-scientific-limitations)
