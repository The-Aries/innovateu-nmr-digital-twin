Numerical thresholds. Bray et al. observed a metal-region peak shifting from about 1050 to 1115 ppm. The team's 1020–1150 ppm integration window covers both reported positions with margin. The team fits a linear spectral background from the flanking 940–980 and 1190–1230 ppm sidebands. It requires a peak inside the window, a peak height at least five times the sideband residual standard deviation, and a signed area above the method sensitivity allowance. These checks screen for peaks that stand above residual background and for areas sensitive to processing. The allowance adds the background residual standard deviation times the window width, the largest area change under sideband shifts of ±10 ppm, and the largest area change under phase rotations of ±10°. The comparison requires the increase to exceed the sum of the two allowances. The exact factor of five, sidebands, and perturbations are exploratory choices. No independent repeatability or detection-limit study supports those settings. Explorer method
