Why start with Figure 5?

Figure 5 combines 16 operando ²³Na NMR acquisitions with 99,841 electrochemical samples from the same sodium-ion full cell during its first formation cycle at 30 mA/g. The archive contains the raw NMR signals, processed spectra, acquisition logs and the electrochemical time series. This combination lets the prototype replay a real experiment and update an estimate when a new spectrum becomes available. Original paper, Figure 5 and Methods · Figure 5 archive

60–75 are Bruker TopSpin experiment numbers (EXPNO). Each number identifies one spectrum acquisition on the same full cell. For example, the audit logs identify the original instrument paths as c_20_2/60 and c_20_2/61. The repository does not explain why this export begins at 60. Each acquisition window lasts about 30 minutes. Numbers are identifiers. Use audit timestamps for timing. Bruker TopSpin processing reference

The audit log provides absolute UTC times for NMR. The electrochemical file stores elapsed time. Its zero point has only a tentative alignment to the NMR clock. Use the full NMR acquisition window when relating a spectrum to the electrochemical trace. Exact event timing depends on the clock offset and the duration of the acquisition.

What the digital twin can demonstrate

  1. Replay the external measurements. Use the current and terminal voltage to follow the cell through formation.
  2. Update the internal state estimate. When a spectrum becomes available, extract the relative area, peak position and detection status in the metallic-sodium region. Update the estimate when the spectral quality checks support it.
  3. Make the update traceable. Record the acquisition ID, UTC window, processing version, spectral uncertainty and assumed clock offset with each update.

The paper reports that a metallic-sodium peak appeared near 1050 ppm during early charging, shifted toward 1115 ppm and then fell below detection. This feature gives the prototype a physical observation to check against. Figure 6 adds the pristine reference, and Figure 7 provides a comparison under a different charging condition. Original paper, Figures 5–7

Figure 5 supports offline replay and measurement-driven state updates for one cell and one cycle. The spectrum provides a relative metallic-sodium signal. Absolute sodium mass requires a separate calibration, spatial morphology requires imaging, and prediction across cells requires independent validation.
