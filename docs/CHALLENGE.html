Challenge: NMR Quantum Engineering: From Spins to a Health Digital Twin — for People, Devices and Objects. Partners: Ulm University, CTU Prague, TU/e.

Input: Starter pack with synthetic MRI and spectroscopy data, an interactive MRI Data Explorer, and a challenge dossier with worked examples.
