InnovateU 2026 — NMR Digital Twin

Private project workspace for the NMR Quantum Engineering challenge (20–21 November 2026, KICU Ulm).

Scope

The challenge asks teams to demonstrate a traceable baseline-to-follow-up update, distinguish physical change from measurement effects, communicate uncertainty, and propose the next measurement. The organizer's [dossier v1.6](MRILocalSpectroscopyHealthDigitalTwinHackathonDossierv1.6.pdf) specifies the deliverables and permits public and synthetic data.

[Project plan and decisions](PROJECT_PLAN.md) maps the four tracks and submission tasks to the Birmingham Figure 5 case.

Project files

  • [Birmingham Explorer](MRIDataExplorer_Birmingham.html) follows the measured ²³Na series and the corresponding electrochemical record for the main case. The cross-device time offset is provisional.
  • [Bruker Explorer](MRIDataExplorer_Bruker.html) reads and compares TopSpin spectra. The Cambridge lithium-metal dataset provides the microwave on/off comparison.
  • [Organizer's MRI Data Explorer](MRIDataExplorer_v1.0-rc5.html) opens the synthetic MRI/MRS teaching case.
  • [Chinese materials index](translations/zh/index.html) links the translations to the organizer's original documents.
  • [Organizer's StarterPack archive](MRIHackathonDataScienceStarterPackv0.3.zip) contains the full synthetic teaching package. Extract it to restore the MRI and MRS example files alongside those already in the StarterPack directory.
  • [Bray et al. (2020)](references/papers/Brayetal2020OperandoSodiumIonBatteryNMR.pdf) is the paper for the main case. See the [Birmingham case walkthrough](references/zh/birminghamdigitaltwinexplainedzh.md) and [background references](references/zh/batterymrreferences_zh.md).
